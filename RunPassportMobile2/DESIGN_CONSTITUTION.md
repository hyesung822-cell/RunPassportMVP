# RUNPASSPORT DESIGN CONSTITUTION v1.0

> 이 문서는 RunPassport의 모든 디자인 결정의 기준점이다.
> 화면을 만들 때마다 이 질문 하나로 판단한다:
> **"이 화면이 'What memory did I collect today?'라는 감정을 주는가?"**

---

## PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

Samsung Health records the body.
Strava records performance.
RunPassport collects memories.

**Tagline: "Your running life, collected."**

---

## ADULT CUTE PRINCIPLES

RunPassport must appeal to adults, not children.

Reference emotional triggers:
- Disney adults
- Animal Crossing players
- Japanese stationery lovers
- Ghibli fans
- Pokémon Sleep users

People love cute things because:
- they feel safe
- they reduce stress
- they provide companionship
- they create rituals
- they encourage collecting
- they build emotional attachment

RunPassport should feel like opening a tiny world that belongs only to the user.

**Never infantilize.**
No childish colors.
No kindergarten aesthetics.
No exaggerated mascots.

Cute must feel:
- cozy
- nostalgic
- personal
- handcrafted
- quietly joyful

---

## CORE DESIGN METAPHORS

Everything in the app belongs to one of these worlds:

✉ Mail
📮 Post Office
🛂 Passport
📔 Traveler's Notebook
🏕 Tiny Adventure Journal
🎫 Stickers
🪪 Identity Cards
🕊 PICO's Deliveries

**Avoid completely:**

🏆 trophies
🥇 medals
📈 analytics dashboards
⚔ competition
🔥 streak addiction mechanics

---

|--------|---------|

---

## CORE LOOP

```
Run
→ Journey Complete
→ 📬 New Mail
→ Open Envelope
→ Card Reveal
→ Passport Updated
→ PICO Celebration
```

---

## VISUAL RULES

**REMOVE:**
- dark luxury themes
- premium gold accents
- corporate dashboards
- enterprise UI
- trophy systems
- heavy statistics-first layouts

**PREFER:**
- cream backgrounds
- rounded shapes
- sticker aesthetics
- paper textures
- soft shadows
- handwritten accents
- cozy motion
- small delightful interactions

---

## PICO PRINCIPLES

PICO is:
- a small post bird
- a memory companion
- a passport keeper

PICO never:
- evolves
- fights
- levels up
- becomes a pet simulator
- requires feeding

PICO delivers:
- memories
- cards
- annual letters
- encouragement
- discoveries

---

## FINAL OBJECTIVE

The product must create feelings that users cannot get from:
- Samsung Health
- Apple Health
- Strava
- Nike Run Club

The question after a run should never be:
**"How fast was I?"**

The question should become:
**"What memory did I collect today?"**
